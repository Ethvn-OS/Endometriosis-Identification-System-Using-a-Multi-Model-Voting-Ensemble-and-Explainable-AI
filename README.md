# Endometriosis-Identification-System-Using-a-Multi-Model-Voting-Ensemble-and-Explainable-AI
Endometriosis is a chronic gynecological disease that is often diagnosed years after symptom onset because of nonspecific clinical manifestations and the limitations of existing diagnostic approaches. This study evaluates a multi-model soft-voting transfer-learning approach using ResNet50, EfficientNetV2S, and DenseNet121 with Grad-CAM explanations.

The primary development experiment uses harmonized T2-weighted slices from the UT-EndoMRI endometriosis cohort and the MOGaMBO cervical-cancer cohort. MOGaMBO is a proxy comparator, not a healthy or clinically confirmed endometriosis-negative cohort. Results therefore measure discrimination between these source cohorts and cannot by themselves establish clinical endometriosis-versus-healthy performance. A collaborating-hospital cohort remains reserved for external evaluation after its MRI sequences and reference labels are verified.

Source volumes are deduplicated by decompressed voxel-content hash before the patient-level split. Reported development counts are therefore unique source volumes linked to dataset patient IDs; independent identity verification is outside the available metadata.
